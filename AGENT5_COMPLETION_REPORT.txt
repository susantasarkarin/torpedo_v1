================================================================================
STATISTICAL METHODS
================================================================================

Chi-Squared Test:
----------------
Purpose: Test if conversion rates are independent of variant
Formula: χ² = Σ((Observed - Expected)² / Expected)
Interpretation: p < 0.05 indicates significant difference

Z-Test for Proportions:
-----------------------
Purpose: Compare two proportions with standard error
Formula: z = (p₁ - p₂) / SE
SE = sqrt(p_pool * (1 - p_pool) * (1/n₁ + 1/n₂))
Interpretation: |z| > 1.96 significant at 95% confidence (two-tailed)

Wilson Score Confidence Interval:
---------------------------------
Purpose: Calculate CI for conversion rate
Formula: CI = (p + z²/2n ± z*sqrt(p(1-p)/n + z²/4n²)) / (1 + z²/n)
Advantage: More accurate for small samples and edge cases

Sample Size Requirements:
------------------------
- Minimum: 100 per variant (configurable)
- Recommended: 200+ for reliable results
- Formula: n = (z²*p*(1-p)) / E² where E = margin of error
