BUSINESS UNIT: Survey Fieldwork (SFW) — Torpedo Research
SLUG: survey_fieldwork
BASKET_CODE: A

DESCRIPTION:
Torpedo Research's Survey Fieldwork division delivers end-to-end quantitative data collection
for market research agencies, panel providers, and research operations teams globally.
We operate a proprietary online panel of 2M+ respondents across 40+ countries, specialising
in hard-to-reach B2B and consumer segments. Our CATI, CAWI, and hybrid field capabilities
allow us to meet complex incidence rate requirements other suppliers routinely decline.

CORE CAPABILITIES:
- Online panel recruitment and management (B2B and B2C)
- CATI (telephone interviews) in 15+ languages
- CAWI (web-assisted interviews) with mobile-first surveys
- Low-incidence and niche B2B sample (IT decision-makers, C-suite, professionals)
- Omnibus surveys (weekly and monthly)
- International fieldwork coordination (simultaneous multi-country projects)
- Real-time data quality monitoring (speeders, straight-liners, open-end validation)
- API-based sample delivery for programmatic buyers

IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE:
- Market research agencies and boutique research firms
- Panel companies looking for supplemental sample
- Corporate insights teams running quantitative studies
- Research operations leads managing vendor relationships
- Data science teams requiring primary research inputs

PAIN POINTS WE SOLVE:
- Low incidence rates causing project delays and cost overruns
- Vendor dropouts mid-field leaving projects stranded
- Poor data quality from low-cost panel providers
- Inability to field niche or professional B2B segments at scale
- Slow turnaround on urgent tracker studies

VALUE PROPOSITION:
We complete what others quote and decline. With a 97% on-time completion rate and a
dedicated project manager per study, we eliminate the fieldwork anxiety that plagues
research ops teams. Our niche B2B panel gives you access to the segments your current
suppliers cannot reach — at competitive CPIs without sacrificing quality.
