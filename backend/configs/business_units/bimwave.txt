BUSINESS UNIT: BIMwave — AEC Digital Intelligence
SLUG: bimwave
BASKET_CODE: C

DESCRIPTION:
BIMwave is Torpedo's built-environment technology arm, providing BIM consulting, coordination,
and digital workflow implementation for architecture, engineering, construction, and real
estate development firms. We bridge the gap between BIM adoption intent and operational
execution — from Revit implementation to IFC-compliant delivery pipelines.

CORE CAPABILITIES:
- BIM strategy and implementation consulting (ISO 19650, UK BIM Level 2/3)
- Revit family creation, template development, and content libraries
- 3D coordination and clash detection (Navisworks, BIM 360/ACC)
- BIM execution plan (BEP) development and project setup
- Point cloud to BIM (Scan-to-BIM) for existing building surveys
- MEP BIM coordination (mechanical, electrical, plumbing)
- As-built and digital twin delivery
- BIM training and capability uplift for AEC teams
- GIS and infrastructure BIM for civil and infrastructure projects

IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE:
- Architecture and design firms adopting Revit-based workflows
- Construction and project management companies requiring BIM coordination
- MEP consultants managing complex building services coordination
- Real estate developers mandating BIM on new-build projects
- Infrastructure and civil engineering firms transitioning to BIM
- Facilities management companies requiring digital twin delivery

PAIN POINTS WE SOLVE:
- BIM projects failing due to poor upfront coordination and file structure
- In-house teams lacking Revit expertise, causing project bottlenecks
- Clash-ridden models discovered late in construction causing costly rework
- No clear BIM Execution Plan, leading to data inconsistency across disciplines
- Inability to deliver ISO 19650-compliant Common Data Environments to clients

VALUE PROPOSITION:
BIMwave treats BIM not as a software problem but as a project delivery problem.
Our team has delivered BIM coordination on projects valued at over £500M across
the UK, Middle East, and Australia. We embed within your project team at the right
stage — whether that is pre-design, detailed design, or construction — and we own
outcomes, not just outputs. Every model we touch is deliverable, coordinated, and
client-ready.
