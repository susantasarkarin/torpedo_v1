BUSINESS UNIT: Cogentix Research — Insights & Analytics
SLUG: cogentix
BASKET_CODE: B

DESCRIPTION:
Cogentix Research is Torpedo's brand intelligence and consumer insights arm, delivering
custom qualitative and quantitative research programmes for brand, marketing, and strategy
teams at FMCG, retail, pharma, media, and financial services companies.
We design studies from scratch, field them globally, and translate raw data into
executive-ready strategic intelligence — not just charts.

CORE CAPABILITIES:
- Brand health tracking (awareness, consideration, purchase intent, NPS)
- Ad effectiveness and campaign evaluation (pre/post measurement)
- Concept testing and innovation research (product, pack, comms)
- Customer satisfaction and loyalty measurement (CX programmes)
- Segmentation and targeting analysis
- U&A studies (Usage & Attitudes)
- Qual: depth interviews, focus groups, ethnography, online communities
- Syndicated category intelligence reports
- Cross-country benchmark studies (14+ markets)

IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE:
- Insights and consumer intelligence managers at FMCG/CPG companies
- Brand strategy directors at marketing agencies
- Market research managers at pharmaceutical and healthcare firms
- Customer experience leads at banks, insurance, and fintech companies
- Product teams needing consumer validation before launch

PAIN POINTS WE SOLVE:
- Research agencies that only deliver data, not insight or recommendations
- Slow turnaround on brand tracking that makes results stale by the time they arrive
- Difficulty connecting consumer insight to commercial decisions
- Inconsistent methodologies across markets making global benchmarking unreliable
- Over-reliance on large consultancies with high cost and low flexibility

VALUE PROPOSITION:
We are an insight partner, not a data vendor. Cogentix Research combines rigorous
methodology with commercial fluency — our researchers have worked inside the brand teams
they now advise. Every deliverable comes with a so-what layer: our recommendations are
testable, commercially grounded, and written for the boardroom, not the research silo.
